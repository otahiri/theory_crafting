# Algorithm
bm25 is an algorithm that specialize in ranking a set of docs by how relevant they are to a query, it uses term frequency, document length to rank the different documents.
the formula is a direct upgrade of the tf-idf.
the tf idf algo had a huge flaw say my question is wait is a transformer, and i have a document that is directly relevant to my question and has the exact thing i am looking for,
and a document that has transformer repeated 10000 times, even tho the former is more relevant to me the tf-idf will score the latter higher just because it has the word transformer so many time.
the bm25 algo fixed this by introducing a punishment for long docs and setting a hard ceiling to how effective the term frequency will have on the result.

## TF-IDF
tf-idf search algorithm was a simple algo that would take the term frequency of each word in the query and devide it by how many words are in the entire documents this score shows how common a word is in a document.
